I am Niloy Hawladar, currently pursuing my M.S. at Università degli Studi di Padova. My research investigates evolutionary and eco-evolutionary responses to global environmental change, integrating macroecological datasets and quantitative analytical approaches to understand biodiversity dynamics. I collaborate with the Global Change Ecology Lab at Monash University (Melbourne) under Dr Shawan Chowdhury.
